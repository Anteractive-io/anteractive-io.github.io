Privacy Policy for Talking Indie

Developer: Anteractive  |  Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Anteractive ("we", "our", or "us") develops mobile applications and interactive games. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application Talking Indie (the "App"). By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Talking Indie is designed to be enjoyed without creating an account or sharing personal details. We do not directly collect, store, sell, or share personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, photos, or precise GPS location.

2. Microphone & Audio Processing

Talking Indie features an interactive 3D virtual pet that listens to and repeats speech in a stylized voice.

  • Real-Time On-Device Processing: Audio captured from your device's microphone is processed strictly in real time on your device to compute volume envelopes for lip-sync animation and apply voice pitch shifting.
  • Zero Recording or External Transmission: We do not record, save, store, or transmit your voice or audio data to any external server or third party. Once playback finishes, the temporary audio buffer is immediately cleared from device memory.
  • Permission Control: Microphone access (RECORD_AUDIO) is requested solely for this interactive feature. You may deny or revoke microphone permission at any time through your device's system settings.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Talking Indie displays optional, user-initiated rewarded video advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

To deliver, measure, and prevent fraud on advertisements, Google AdMob may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable technical and diagnostic data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • General, coarse location (country or city level, inferred from IP address)
  • App performance, crash diagnostics, and ad interaction metrics (e.g., views, clicks, and frequency capping)

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). To learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services

4. Children’s Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Talking Indie is designed as safe, family-friendly entertainment suitable for audiences of all ages.

  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
  • The App does not include user accounts, chat rooms, leaderboards, or public social profiles.
  • In-game items can be unlocked through normal gameplay or via optional rewarded video ads that require explicit user interaction.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has inadvertently provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

Because Talking Indie does not require user registration or collect personal data, no user profiles or personal data are stored on remote servers. All game progress, unlocked items, and pet customizations are stored locally on your device.

Deleting Your Data: You can permanently delete all locally stored App data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling Talking Indie.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our App, features, or applicable legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
