Privacy Policy for Infinite Cards

Developer: Anteractive • Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Anteractive ("we", "our", or "us") develops mobile applications and interactive puzzle games. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play our mobile application Infinite Cards (the "App").

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Infinite Cards is designed as a relaxing, offline-first puzzle experience. We respect your privacy and believe in minimal data access:

  • No Account Creation: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details to play the game.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, harvest, store, sell, or share personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.
  • No Sensor Access: Infinite Cards does not request access to your microphone, camera, location services, or biometric hardware.

2. Local Device Storage

All gameplay progress and game settings are stored strictly locally on your device using Android's standard local storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Level progression (unlocked levels and completed levels).
  • Best tap records per level.
  • User preferences (theme selection, light/dark mode, sound effects toggle, meditation background music toggle, haptic feedback toggle, and tutorial completion state).

This data is kept exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to, uploaded to, or backed up on external servers controlled by Anteractive.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Infinite Cards displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support development and keep the game free to play:

  • Banner Ads: A non-intrusive banner ad displayed at the top of gameplay and tutorial screens.
  • User-Initiated Rewarded Ads: Optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch in exchange for an in-game hint or an auto-solve solution. These ads are never triggered automatically without your explicit consent.

To serve ads, measure ad performance, detect invalid activity, and prevent fraud, Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain diagnostic and technical device information, such as:

  • Mobile advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • Coarse location data (city or country level derived from IP address).
  • General device information (device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and mobile network carrier).
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, impressions, clicks, and frequency capping).

You can manage, reset, or opt out of personalized advertising on your Android device at any time by visiting Settings → Google → Ads.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services.

4. Google Play In-App Updates

Infinite Cards integrates the official Google Play In-App Update API. When your device is connected to the internet, the App performs a background check directly with the Google Play Store to determine if a newer version is available. This process is handled securely by Google Play services and does not transmit personal data to Anteractive.

5. Children’s Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Infinite Cards is designed as a peaceful, family-friendly geometric puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages:

  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable by law).
  • The App does not contain user accounts, chat rooms, open message boards, social media integration, or public leaderboards.
  • In-game rewards (hints and solutions) require explicit user clicks and contain child-appropriate ad filters where applicable.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Infinite Cards does not collect or transmit personal information to remote servers, no user profiles or personal records exist on our servers.

Deleting Your Data: You can permanently delete all locally stored game progress, completed levels, best tap counts, and saved settings at any time by:

  • Opening Settings inside Infinite Cards and tapping "Reset All Progress", OR
  • Going to your device's Settings → Apps → Infinite Cards → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage, OR
  • Uninstalling the App from your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect updates to our game features, third-party libraries, or applicable regulations. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
